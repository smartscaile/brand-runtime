# Surface guidelines

Read the section for the requested surface after loading the universal foundation and the selected Brand Pack context.

## Site

- Build a narrative sequence, not a uniform stack of interchangeable sections.
- Give each section a thesis, its strongest supporting content or evidence, and an intentional transition to the next section.
- Introduce interaction only when it improves understanding, confidence, qualification, or conversion.
- Treat the hero, evidence, product or offer, objections, and action as different compositional problems.
- Keep primary actions direct and stable on mobile.
- Validate loading, responsive imagery, keyboard behavior, form states, and long content.

## Product

- Prioritize task completion, state clarity, and information density over campaign-style decoration.
- Make navigation, hierarchy, primary action, system status, errors, empty states, loading, and confirmation explicit.
- Keep repeated components behaviorally consistent while allowing content-driven composition in overview and education surfaces.
- Validate keyboard, focus, touch targets, zoom, destructive actions, and realistic data extremes.

## Document

- Preserve complete content and global order unless editing is requested.
- Choose continuous flow for natural screen reading and a page canvas only when physical preview or fixed-page export matters.
- Derive page geometry from the requested format. Share geometry between screen and `@page`; keep workspace shadows and sheet gaps screen-only.
- On mobile, use fluid width and natural height unless fixed-page simulation is explicitly required.
- Keep headings with their first content block. Avoid stranded headings, ambiguous continuations, and unnecessary splits in short lists, comparisons, or callouts.
- Paginate by units of meaning instead of reproducing source page breaks mechanically.
- Verify exported page count, geometry, overflow, edge insets, widows, orphans, and reading continuity.

## Presentation

- Preserve content and global order unless editing is requested, then paginate by meaning.
- Give each slide one dominant message and enough evidence to support it.
- Size text-bearing regions from known content and rebalance the slide instead of clipping or shrinking text arbitrarily.
- Keep related labels, icons, charts, and explanations visually attached.
- Vary slide composition according to content while preserving recognizable brand constants.
- Render every slide and review sequence, density, overflow, alignment, and legibility at presentation distance.

## Shared editorial rules

- Inventory declared iconography before creating definition, capability, status, or action blocks.
- Use whitespace to establish grouping without creating dead zones between related elements.
- Use cards, dividers, and callouts only when they improve grouping, comparison, status, or emphasis.
- Prefer typography, data, imagery, or a plain note when a generic decorative container adds no meaning.
- Maintain mapped component insets after final wrapping; zero overflow alone is not sufficient.
