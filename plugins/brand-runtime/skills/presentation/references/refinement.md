# Slide refinement

## Diagnose before styling

Aplique o diagnóstico e o refinamento pela causa de `../../brand/references/design-foundation.md`. Em slides, acrescente a relação com a narrativa vizinha, a distância de leitura e a paridade entre HTML e PDF quando PDF for solicitado. As escalas abaixo especializam a crítica comum, sem criar uma autoridade concorrente.

## Three-scale critique

### Thumbnail scale

Inspect the current slide beside its neighbors at a size where body copy cannot be read. Confirm that the dominant mass, focal point, density, and negative space create the intended batch rhythm. Repeated silhouettes without a semantic reason indicate a composition problem even when family labels differ.

### Full-slide scale

Follow the actual reading path at presentation distance. Confirm one dominant entry, a legible transition into explanation or evidence, and a clear exit toward the next idea. Check that containers, dividers, labels, and decoration reveal relationships rather than manufacture structure.

### Detail scale

Inspect typography, alignment, crop, annotation, source treatment, units, and repeated component geometry. Correct local craft only after the thumbnail and full-slide passes are coherent.

A layout does not pass because it is technically valid, different from its neighbor, or assigned a different family name. Technical QA supplies evidence; a person still approves visual maturity and brand fit.

## Three-direction method

Offer alternatives only when the user's intent leaves a material design choice unresolved.

### Conservative

Preserve the existing structure. Correct spacing, type scale, alignment, density, and local contrast.

### Editorial

Recompose the hierarchy and pacing while preserving the underlying information and approved copy.

### Structural

Change the information model, visual form, or slide sequence because the existing structure is the problem.

Make every direction meaningfully different. Recommend one. Do not ask the user to choose among arbitrary implementation details.

## Refinement loop

1. Name the issue in one sentence.
2. State what must remain unchanged.
3. Select or propose a direction.
4. Implement one coherent pass.
5. Render the full slide.
6. Inspect the thumbnail sequence.
7. Follow the full-slide reading path.
8. Inspect the critical detail.
9. Check neighboring slides and repeated components.
10. Record a reusable project pattern only when the user asks to remember it.

## Signs of artificial or template-like output

Use os diagnósticos da base comum. No deck, observe também métricas comprimidas como dashboard quando a audiência precisa acompanhar uma tese à distância, marcadores de capítulo inconsistentes e screenshots ilegíveis. Corrija edição e relações antes de decorar. Um nome diferente de família não corrige silhuetas equivalentes.

## Acceptance

Treat explicit positive feedback as acceptance of the current direction, not automatic permission to turn it into a universal rule. Preserve the accepted slide unless the user asks for further change. Keep rejected directions out of later iterations unless the user reopens them.
